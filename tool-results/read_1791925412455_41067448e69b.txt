     1→# Product Requirements Document
     2→## Bilingual Online Menu SaaS
     3→
     4→**Product Type:** Multi-tenant SaaS  
     5→**Primary Languages:** Persian (RTL) + English (LTR)  
     6→**Target Businesses:** Restaurants, cafés, fast-food restaurants, bakeries, juice bars, hotels, food courts, catering businesses, and similar food-service businesses  
     7→**Platforms:** Web, PWA, Mobile Web  
     8→**Primary Access Method:** QR Code + direct URL  
     9→**Architecture:** Multi-tenant SaaS  
    10→**Initial Deployment:** Single-instance capable, horizontally scalable
    11→
    12→---
    13→
    14→# 1. Product Vision
    15→
    16→Create a modern SaaS platform that allows food-service businesses to create, manage, customize, and publish a professional online menu without requiring technical knowledge.
    17→
    18→Each business receives its own public menu URL and QR code.
    19→
    20→Example:
    21→
    22→```text
    23→https://menu.example.com/cafe-nova
    24→```
    25→
    26→Customers scan the QR code at the table and immediately see the restaurant's menu.
    27→
    28→The system must support:
    29→
    30→- Persian and English content
    31→- RTL/LTR interfaces
    32→- Categories and menu items
    33→- Images
    34→- Prices
    35→- Variants
    36→- Add-ons
    37→- Availability
    38→- Dietary information
    39→- Search
    40→- Featured items
    41→- Promotional items
    42→- Restaurant branding
    43→- QR codes
    44→- Analytics
    45→- Multi-tenant administration
    46→- Multiple branches
    47→- Role-based access control
    48→
    49→The product should feel closer to a **digital storefront** than a simple PDF menu.
    50→
    51→---
    52→
    53→# 2. Problem Statement
    54→
    55→Traditional restaurant menus have several problems:
    56→
    57→- Printing is expensive.
    58→- Price changes require reprinting.
    59→- Customers cannot easily switch languages.
    60→- PDF menus are often difficult to navigate on mobile.
    61→- Menu images may be low quality.
    62→- Restaurants have no useful analytics.
    63→- Different branches may have different menus.
    64→- Updating availability is inconvenient.
    65→- QR-code menus are often visually poor.
    66→- Restaurants usually need a developer to make significant changes.
    67→
    68→The SaaS should solve these problems with a simple management dashboard and a fast mobile-first customer experience.
    69→
    70→---
    71→
    72→# 3. Goals
    73→
    74→## Primary Goals
    75→
    76→1. Allow a business to create a digital menu in minutes.
    77→2. Allow menu content to exist independently in Persian and English.
    78→3. Provide automatic RTL/LTR rendering.
    79→4. Provide a mobile-first customer experience.
    80→5. Generate QR codes automatically.
    81→6. Allow restaurants to update prices and availability instantly.
    82→7. Support multiple branches.
    83→8. Support restaurant branding.
    84→9. Provide basic analytics.
    85→10. Provide subscription-based SaaS billing.
    86→11. Support future languages without redesigning the data model.
    87→
    88→## Secondary Goals
    89→
    90→- PWA installation
    91→- SEO-friendly public menus
    92→- Social sharing
    93→- WhatsApp/Telegram sharing
    94→- Table-specific QR codes
    95→- Promotional campaigns
    96→- Ordering integration in future versions
    97→- POS integration
    98→- Digital signage
    99→

   305→# 9. Bilingual Content Model
   306→
   307→Every translatable entity should store language-specific fields.
   308→
   309→Example:
   310→
   311→```json
   312→{
   313→  "name": {
   314→    "fa": "چیزبرگر",
   315→    "en": "Cheeseburger"
   316→  },
   317→  "description": {
   318→    "fa": "برگر گوشت با پنیر چدار",
   319→    "en": "Beef burger with cheddar cheese"
   320→  }
   321→}
   322→```
   323→
   324→Do NOT create separate products for each language.
   325→
   326→Instead:
   327→
   328→```text
   329→MenuItem
   330→ ├── name.fa
   331→ ├── name.en
   332→ ├── description.fa
   333→ └── description.en
   334→```
   335→
   336→This keeps both language versions tied to the same product.
